# Window CRM Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Window CRM ("the app") is a tool for window cleaners to manage their clients, quotes and job schedule. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

## Information we store

- **Account information:** your email address and password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
- **Business records you enter:** client names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses and notes, as well as quotes, jobs, prices and reminder preferences.

This information is entered by you and is used only to provide the app's features to you.

## How it is stored

Your data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our database and authentication provider. Access is restricted so that each account can only read and change its own records.

Job reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any push notification service.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking.
- We do not use your data for any purpose other than running the app for you.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account** in the app. This immediately removes your account and all clients, quotes, jobs and settings stored in it.
